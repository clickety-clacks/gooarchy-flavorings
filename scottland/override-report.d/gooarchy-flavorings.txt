## Gooarchy touchpad defaults
Reason: Gooarchy enables touchpad tapping and dragging for its Scottland session.

- Keys: touchpad tap-to-click and tap-and-drag
  Was: No Gooarchy tap-to-click or tap-and-drag setting
  Now: Tap-to-click and tap-and-drag are enabled in the Scottland session
  Origin: Gooarchy flavoring

## Gooarchy screenshot shortcuts
Reason: Gooarchy uses the user's chosen Pictures folder, including localized or moved folders.

- Keys: Print and Shift+Print
  Was: Scottland saves both screenshots under ~/Pictures as wayfire-<timestamp>.png
  Now: Print saves under the XDG Pictures folder as screenshot-<timestamp>.png; Shift+Print saves a selected region there
  Origin: Gooarchy flavoring
